Holy Quran: Read & Listen ("we", "our", or "us") does not collect, store or share any personal data on a server. This Privacy Policy explains what the app does — and, just as importantly, what it does not do — with information about you.

01Information collection and use

Holy Quran: Read & Listen does not require you to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the app. We do not operate a server that receives or stores data about you.

A. Location data

If you choose to share your location, the app reads your device’s approximate location solely to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, on the device itself. That location is never sent to us or stored beyond the current calculation. You can skip this entirely and pick your city from a list instead.

B. Public API usage

The app fetches Quran text, translations, tafsir and recitation audio from public, freely available Quran content providers — AlQuran.cloud and Quran.com. These requests carry only the standard technical information any web request carries (such as your device’s IP address); no account identifier is sent, because the app has none.

02What stays on your device

To work offline and remember your preferences, the app stores the following only on your own phone. It is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Bookmarked ayahs, reading progress, streaks and khatam (completion) tracking
  • Your chosen theme, translation language, tafsir and reading preferences
  • Downloaded recitation audio, for offline listening
  • Your reminder and prayer-alert schedule, kept and triggered entirely on the device

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone’s Settings, permanently deletes all of this. We have no copy of it anywhere.

03Data safety & security

We value your trust. To be specific about what that means in practice:

  • No personal identifiers are collected by the app, directly or indirectly.
  • The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no advertising identifier.
  • All network communication takes place over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age — because it does not collect personal data from anyone at all.

About this website

This website is a static, informational site about the app. It does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not collect personal information through forms. Fonts are served from this site rather than from a third party.

As with any website, the hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP addresses in server logs, in order to deliver pages securely.
