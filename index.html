Introduction

Reading, kept simple.

Holy Quran is an Android application for reading, listening to and learning the Holy Quran. It is built around a single idea: opening the Quran on your phone should be easy, and using it every day should feel calm and clear, never crowded.

The app keeps its interface plain and its navigation straightforward, with no account, no advertising and nothing watching how you use it — so that anyone can pick it up and begin reading without having to learn how it works first.

Features

What the app offers

  • Read & listen

    The Mushaf page by page, plus recitation by well-known reciters.

  • Calm, uncluttered design

    Eight light and dark themes, built to keep attention on the words.

  • Prayer times & Qibla

    Accurate times and direction, calculated right on your phone.

  • Works fully offline

    Download a recitation once, then read and listen with no internet.

  • No sign-up, no ads

    Open the app and start reading straight away.
